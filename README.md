# World Explorer: Continents and Oceans

A study app and printable pack for Grade 3 World Geography (Virginia SOL 3.2 and 3.3): the 7 continents and 5 oceans, the equator, the prime meridian and hemispheres, major rivers and mountains, and how geography affects daily life.

- **App:** open `index.html` (works on phone, tablet, or computer, fully offline). Includes a flat map, a spinnable globe, map/word/feature quizzes, bonus facts, and a practice test.
- **Printables:** `printables/World-Geography-Study-Guide.pdf` (12 pages, double-sided) and `printables/World-Geography-Answer-Key.pdf`.

Map data: Natural Earth via world-atlas (public domain).
